# Global existence of smooth self-similar quenching profiles

A rigorous version of Theorem 2.1 of Gimperlein–He–Lacey, *Quenching for a semi-linear wave equation for
micro-electro-mechanical systems*, Proc. R. Soc. A 478 (2022) 20220490: existence of a sequence of smooth,
radial, global self-similar profiles `v_k` of `u_tt = Δu − u⁻²` in dimensions 2 ≤ n ≤ 7, obtained by combining

* the sonic-point analysis of Merle–Raphaël–Rodnianski–Szeftel at the light cone `η = 1`
  (non-resonance `α = n/2 + 1/6 ∉ ℕ`, analytic branch through the cone, singular coefficient `β(c)`),
* a quantitative inner/outer matching in the spirit of Collot–Raphaël–Szeftel around the exact singular
  profile `a η^{2/3}`,
* a weighted flux identity (the ODE analogue of the Merle–Zaag energy identity) for monotonicity and the
  growth `v ~ b η^{2/3}` at infinity.

Files: `self-similar-existence.tex` / `.pdf` (the note), `numerics/` (two-point shooting scripts and the
table of computed profiles).
